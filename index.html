HONEST Snapshot Viewer

Feed snapshots from squidKid-deluxe/HONEST-feeds-live . Publishers are detected automatically from the repo; snapshots are served straight from raw.githubusercontent.com.

Pick a publisher, then pick a snapshot. The tables match the render_price_logs.py output: DEX, CEX, FOREX, and the final feed.

Snapshots whose prices.time.unix is more than 7200 seconds old are treated as stale.
